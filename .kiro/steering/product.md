# Product Steering: TaaS Solutions Digital Delivery Platform

## Product Identity

**Name:** TaaS Solutions  
**Full Name:** Talent as a Service Solutions  
**Tagline:** Turning Skills into Income

## Product Purpose

TaaS Solutions is a **managed ICT solutions and digital delivery platform** that connects organisations requiring ICT solutions with verified emerging technology professionals through governed, multidisciplinary Talent Pods.

## What TaaS Solutions Is

- **ICT Solutions Provider** – Delivers defined business outcomes for clients
- **Managed Delivery Organisation** – Assembles and governs multidisciplinary teams
- **Client Opportunity Platform** – Intake, qualification, and project management
- **Verified Talent Network** – Skills verification and capabilities matching
- **Talent Pod Assembly System** – Team formation and assignment
- **Project Delivery Workspace** – Collaborative delivery environment
- **Quality Assurance Platform** – Internal review and client acceptance
- **Commercial Operations Platform** – Proposals, contracts, invoicing, payouts
- **Professional Experience Platform** – Verifiable evidence of completed work
- **Youth Livelihood Platform** – Paid pathways to sustainable careers
- **Partner Ecosystem Platform** – Collaboration with education and industry partners

## What TaaS Solutions Is NOT

- Basic corporate marketing website
- Public jobs board
- Recruitment agency placing individual candidates
- CV database or resume aggregator
- Temporary employment agency
- Unmanaged staffing platform
- Open freelancer bidding marketplace
- Lowest-bid competition system
- Social media network
- Training-only platform
- Charity portal
- Automated AI hiring system with opaque decisions

## Business Model

TaaS Solutions delivers **defined business outcomes** through **managed Talent Pods**, not individual contractor placement.

**Client Value:**

- Defined scope and outcomes
- Managed delivery with governance
- Quality assurance
- Fixed engagement model
- Access to emerging but verified talent

**Talent Value:**

- Paid project experience
- Mentorship and oversight
- Portfolio building
- Skills development
- Transparent progression
- Pathway to sustainable careers

**TaaS Value:**

- Project revenue
- Talent network growth
- Case study development
- Social impact evidence
- Sustainable business model

## User Roles

### Public Tier

- **Public Visitor** – View services, request solutions, apply to talent network, view approved case studies

### Talent Tier

- **Talent Applicant** – Register, build profile, submit for verification
- **Verified Talent** – Participate in pods, deliver work, build experience, view payouts

### Client Tier

- **Client Member** – Submit opportunities, view organisation projects, upload documents, participate in clarifications
- **Client Approver** – Approve proposals, accept deliverables, approve change requests, review invoices

### Delivery Tier

- **Delivery Lead** – Manage assigned pods, coordinate work, review contributions, manage risks and issues, communicate status

### Operations Tier

- **Talent Operations Administrator** – Manage verification, search talent, support progression, manage onboarding
- **Project Operations Administrator** – Review opportunities, capture discovery, create proposals, assemble pods, mobilise projects, monitor delivery
- **Quality Reviewer** – Review deliverables, record findings, approve quality gates, return for rework
- **Finance Administrator** – Create invoices, record payments, create payouts, approve payouts, view audit history

### Platform Tier

- **Platform Administrator** – Manage configuration, roles, permissions, reference data, audit, support users

### Partner Tier

- **Partner/Mentor** – Refer talent, support projects, record mentorship (with governed access)

## Success Principles

Every feature must create measurable value for:

1. **The client** receiving the solution
2. **The participating talent** receiving paid experience and progression
3. **TaaS Solutions** building sustainable delivery capability

Features that do not strengthen the core business lifecycle should not automatically be included.

## Product Constraints

- **Security-first:** All access must be authorised server-side
- **Privacy-first:** Confidential information must be isolated by organisation and role
- **Audit-first:** Sensitive state transitions must create immutable audit events
- **Quality-first:** Deliverables must pass internal review before client review
- **Evidence-first:** Talent progression must be based on verifiable evidence
- **Human-first:** Critical decisions (talent approval, proposal approval, acceptance) require human authorisation

## Key Differentiators

1. **Managed Pods, Not Individual Placement** – Clients receive teams, not CVs
2. **Quality Governance** – Internal QA before client review
3. **Transparent Progression** – Clear criteria and evidence-based advancement
4. **Youth Empowerment** – African youth gaining paid, meaningful experience
5. **Premium Positioning** – Enterprise-grade consultancy, not bargain marketplace

## Open Business Decisions

The following decisions are **unresolved** and must not be silently implemented:

1. **Pricing model** (fixed-price, day-rate, value-based, hybrid)
2. **Talent compensation model** (percentage split, market rate, negotiable, tiered)
3. **Minimum project size**
4. **Legal relationship with talent** (contractor, employee, freelancer)
5. **Talent exclusivity requirements**
6. **Payment timing** (upon client payment, milestone-based, upon closure)
7. **Deliverable rejection handling** (rework cost, change request, dispute)
8. **IP ownership default** (client, TaaS, shared)
9. **Client access to talent profiles** (full, limited, none)
10. **Talent opportunity visibility** (browse, assigned-only, expression of interest)

Where these decisions impact implementation, **stop and seek clarification** rather than proceeding with assumptions.
